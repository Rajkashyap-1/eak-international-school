About EAK International School

Over 28 years of building inquiring, knowledgeable, and caring young leaders motivated to succeed in an interconnected world.

Our Vision

To Be a World Benchmark for Holistic & Ethical K-12 Education

We envision a learning sanctuary where every student discovers their innate potential, develops an agile global mindset, and leads with empathy, scientific rigor, and unyielding integrity.

Our Mission

Empower, Enlighten, and Inspire

To provide a dynamic, student-centric academic environment that combines innovative pedagogy with character development, preparing learners to excel in premier global universities and champion societal well-being.

Foundational Pillars

Our Guiding Core Values

These values govern every interaction, academic lesson, and institutional choice across our campus.

Integrity First

Fostering ethical conduct, honest discourse, and personal accountability in both victory and defeat.

Relentless Curiosity

Encouraging students to question deeply, investigate fearlessly, and turn curiosity into purposeful discovery.

Empathy & Inclusion

Valuing diverse perspectives, promoting mutual respect, and instilling compassion toward society and environment.

Excellence in Action

Striving for our personal best across scholastic achievements, sports, performing arts, and community service.

Our Heritage

Milestones on Our 28-Year Journey

From a humble beginning to a nationally celebrated institution of higher K-12 learning.

1998

Foundation of EAK Academy

Inaugurated with 120 students and 14 dedicated teachers committed to progressive child-centric learning.

2006

Expansion into 20-Acre Modern Campus

Relocated to our current green institutional sector with dedicated science labs, athletic fields, and smart classrooms.

2015

International STEAM Lab & Robotics Wing

Introduced pioneering hands-on robotics, computer programming, and design-thinking studios into the core curriculum.

2021

Global Top 50 Green Schools Recognition

Awarded the National Sustainability Award for zero-waste initiatives, solar power generation, and environmental conservation.

2026

New Horizon of AI & Collaborative Innovation

Inauguration of the Center for Artificial Intelligence, student incubator spaces, and broadened university pathways.
