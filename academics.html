Academic Hierarchy & Curriculum

Discover our comprehensive K-12 learning ladder designed to challenge minds and nurture lifelong scholarship.

Foundational Years (Grades Nursery - 5)

Primary School: Sparking Joy in Learning

In our primary years, children learn through touch, inquiry, and active exploration. Our curriculum integrates phonics, thematic inquiry, numerical confidence, and artistic expression in a supportive environment.

Key Focus Areas:

Phonics & Early Literacy Sensory Math Manipulatives Environmental Discovery Speech & Creative Drama Social-Emotional Habits
Exploratory Years (Grades 6 - 8)

Middle School: Fostering Critical Inquiry

Middle school bridges foundational skills with specialized scientific reasoning and analytical debate. Students engage in practical laboratory experiments, algorithmic coding, and collaborative team research.

Key Focus Areas:

Scientific Method & Wet Labs Foreign Languages (French/German) Computational Thinking & Python World Geography & Civics Competitive Sports & House League
Preparatory Years (Grades 9 - 10)

Secondary Wing: Rigor & Examination Mastery

Prepares students for board examinations with intensive subject depth, regular diagnostic assessments, and mentor-guided study plans, alongside leadership responsibilities in school prefectorial boards.

Key Focus Areas:

National Board Curricula Advanced Mathematics & Calculus Applied Physics, Chem, Bio Model United Nations (MUN) Career Aptitude Mapping
University Pathways (Grades 11 - 12)

Senior Secondary: Dedicated Specialization

High schoolers choose specialized academic tracks tailored to their career goals with personalized college portfolio advising, SAT/competitive exam coaching, and university internship exposure.

Available Academic Streams:

STEM Stream (PCM / PCB / AI) Commerce & Financial Markets Humanities, Law & Liberal Arts Ivy & Global University Placements
Beyond Academics

Co-Curricular Excellence

Real education extends beyond textbooks. We offer over 35 active student-led societies and sports academies.

Robotics & AI Guild

Building autonomous rover prototypes and competing in national FIRST Tech Challenge tournaments.

Debate & Parliamentary Union

Sharpening rhetorical mastery, policy arguments, and participating in Harvard MUN conferences.

Symphonic Orchestra & Choir

Training in Western classical violin, piano, Indian vocal classical, and percussion compositions.

Eco-Warriors Climate Society

Spearheading campus rainwater harvesting, organic horticulture, and regional solar audit campaigns.

Visual Arts & Animation Studio

Oil canvas painting, digital illustration on iPad Pro suites, 3D modeling, and pottery arts.

Aquatics & Martial Arts Club

Certified black-belt Taekwondo training, professional swimming strokes, and water polo team.
