Transparent Steps

The 5-Step Admissions Process

A warm, structured, and clear transition designed to understand your child’s unique needs.

1

Enquiry

Fill out our online inquiry form or visit our admissions desk for a prospectus and initial counseling.

2

Application

Submit the official registration dossier alongside academic transcripts and supporting identification.

3

Interaction / Assessment

Friendly interactive session for Kindergarten/Primary, and concept evaluation for Middle & High School.

4

Selection

Communication of admission offer by email along with scholarship status and fee schedule guidelines.

5

Enrollment

Formal fee payment, document verification, welcome orientation kit distribution, and uniform allocation.

Checklist

Mandatory Documents

  • Official Birth Certificate: Issued by Municipal Corporation or relevant authority (Original & 2 photocopies).
  • Previous Academic Records: Report cards / Mark sheets of the last two academic years (Grade 2 upwards).
  • Transfer Certificate (TC): Countersigned by Education Department / Board officer upon final admission.
  • Passport Size Photographs: 4 colored photos of candidate and 2 each of parents/guardians.
  • Proof of Residence: Passport, Aadhaar, Utility Bill, or Registered Lease Agreement.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scholarships, admission criteria, and deadlines.

What is the teacher-to-student ratio at EAK International?
We maintain an optimal 1:12 mentor-to-student ratio in kindergarten and 1:18 in primary and high school classrooms. This ensures personalized attention, immediate feedback, and tailored academic support for every child.
Are merit scholarships available for exceptional students?
Yes. EAK provides up to 100% academic scholarships for candidates demonstrating exceptional talent in national Olympiads, state/national sports, and meritorious performance in Grade X board examinations.
How are students transported safely to and from campus?
Our GPS-enabled bus fleet covers over 45 arterial routes across the metropolitan region. Each bus features onboard CCTV, speed governors, first-aid kits, and vetted female attendants with live tracking via our parent mobile portal.
Can we schedule an in-person physical tour of the campus?
Campus tours are conducted every Monday through Saturday between 9:00 AM and 2:30 PM. You can book an appointment via our Contact page or simply drop an inquiry using the form above.
What curriculum does the school follow?
We follow an internationally enriched CBSE curriculum integrated with inquiry-based STEM methodologies and global languages (French, German, and Spanish options from Grade 6 upwards).
